# Customer Retention Strategy

## 1. Business Objective

Identify customer segments with higher observed churn
and develop targeted retention actions.

## 2. Priority Segments

### Month-to-month customers show a higher observed churn rate.

Observed pattern:
Approximately 42% churn rate.

Suggested action:
Test incentives for eligible customers to move to
longer-term contracts.

KPI:
Contract Conversion Rate and Churn Rate.

---

### New Customers — 0–6 Months

Observed pattern:
Approximately 53% churn rate.

Suggested action:
Strengthen onboarding and early-stage customer engagement.

KPI:
0–6 Month Churn Rate.

---

### Fiber Optic Customers

Observed pattern:
Approximately 42% churn rate.

Suggested action:
Investigate service experience, pricing and recorded
churn reasons for this segment.

KPI:
Fiber Optic Churn Rate.

---

### Electronic-Check Customers

Observed pattern:
Highest displayed churn rate among payment methods,
at approximately 45%+.

Suggested action:
Investigate payment-related customer characteristics
and experience.

KPI:
Electronic-Check Churn Rate.

---

## 3. High-Risk Customers

The dashboard contains a provided risk classification
based on the dataset's Churn Score.

Suggested action:
Use the classification to prioritize customers for
further investigation or proactive engagement.

## 4. Measurement Plan

Monitor:

- Overall Churn Rate
- Segment-level Churn Rate
- Revenue at Risk
- Contract Conversion Rate
- Customer Retention Rate
- CLTV
