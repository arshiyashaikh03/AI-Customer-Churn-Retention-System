# AI-Assisted Churn Insights

## 1. Overall Churn

- Total customers: approximately 7K
- Churned customers: approximately 2K
- Overall churn rate: 26.54%
- Revenue at risk: 2.86M

## 2. Key Churn Patterns

### Contract

Month-to-month customers show the highest observed churn rate at approximately 42%.

One-year customers show approximately 11% churn, while two-year customers show approximately 3%.

### Tenure Group

The 0–6 month customer group shows the highest observed churn rate at approximately 53%.

Churn decreases across the displayed tenure groups, reaching approximately 9% for customers with 49+ months.

### Internet Service

Fiber optic customers show an observed churn rate of approximately 42%.

DSL customers show approximately 19%, while customers with no internet service show approximately 7%.

### Payment Method

Electronic-check customers show the highest displayed churn rate among the payment methods at approximately 45%+.

## 3. Customer Segments Requiring Attention

- Month-to-month customers
- Customers in the first 0–6 months
- Fiber optic customers
- Electronic-check customers
- Customers classified in the High Risk category

## 4. Possible Business Interpretations

These findings represent observed patterns and do not establish causation.

Possible areas for further investigation include:

- Early customer onboarding and experience
- Contract commitment and retention
- Fiber optic service experience and pricing
- Payment-related customer characteristics
- Customers with higher provided Churn Scores

## 5. Suggested Retention Actions

- Strengthen early-customer onboarding.
- Test incentives for eligible customers to move to longer-term contracts.
- Investigate churn reasons among fiber optic customers.
- Investigate customer characteristics associated with electronic-check payments.
- Prioritize proactive engagement with higher-risk customers.

## 6. KPIs to Monitor

- Churn Rate
- Churned Customer Count
- Revenue at Risk
- New-Customer Churn Rate
- Contract Conversion Rate
- High-Risk Customer Count
- Customer Lifetime Value (CLTV)

## 7. Executive Summary

The analysis shows an overall churn rate of 26.54%, with approximately 2K churned customers and 2.86M in revenue at risk. Higher observed churn is concentrated among month-to-month customers, customers in the first 0–6 months, fiber optic customers, and electronic-check customers. These segments should be investigated further and can be considered for targeted retention initiatives.
