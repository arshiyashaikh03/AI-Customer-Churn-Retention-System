# Business Problem

## Project
AI Customer Churn & Retention System

## Business Objective

Identify the major factors associated with customer churn and develop
an AI-assisted customer retention strategy.

## Business Problem

A telecom company is losing customers and wants to understand why
customers are leaving.

Management wants to identify:

- Which customers are more likely to churn?
- Which customer segments have higher churn?
- Which contracts are associated with higher churn?
- Does customer tenure relate to churn?
- Does monthly charge relate to churn?
- Which services are associated with churn?
- What revenue is associated with customers who have churned?
- What retention strategies could be tested?

## Target Users

- Business Managers
- Customer Retention Team
- Marketing Team
- Customer Service Team
- Business Analysts
