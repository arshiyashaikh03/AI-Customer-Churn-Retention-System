# AI Customer Churn & Retention System

## Project Overview

This project analyses customer churn for a telecom company using Power BI, Power Query and AI-assisted business analysis.

The objective is to identify customer segments with higher observed churn, understand churn patterns, estimate revenue at risk and develop data-driven retention strategies.

---

## Business Problem

Telecom companies lose revenue when customers leave their services.

The goal of this project is to analyze customer data and answer:

- What percentage of customers have churned?
- Which customer segments show higher churn?
- Which contract types have higher observed churn?
- Which tenure groups show higher churn?
- How does churn vary by internet service?
- How does churn vary by payment method?
- What churn reasons are recorded?
- How much revenue is at risk?
- What retention actions can be investigated?
